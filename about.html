About

A person, thinking in public.

I’m Chris. This is a place for ideas that benefit from being made explicit: mathematics I am learning, systems I am trying to understand, arguments I have not finished, and observations that do not fit neatly elsewhere.

The subjects will likely wander. Expect mathematics, embedded systems, medicine, artificial intelligence, philosophy, and the occasional note about building things.

This site is intentionally small. There is no feed to satisfy, no algorithm to appease, and no promise of a posting schedule. The aim is simply to leave some thoughts in a form that can survive their author’s working memory.
