A Blog of Sorts

Hello! This site is best thought of as a diverse blog: I'll write about the mathematics of which I learn, systems I am trying to understand, arguments I have not fully litigated, and observations that do not fit neatly elsewhere. It's possible more creative things become apart of that list, such as poems I have written and artworks I've seen. Be forewarned, I tend to wander about any given subject, and I often have unique quirks in my informal prose.

To give an overview of myself: I currently study at the University of Michigan - Ann Arbor as a student of mathematics. As I wish to graduate with a degree in Honors Mathematics, I'm sure that I'll hash out many academic topics. Furthermore, I have considered myself to be vegan for five years now — the pedantry is warranted if you're familiar with vegan discourse — which greatly influences my person, mainly by proxy, enabling my delving into normative ethics! Ergo, I am prone to engage in some superficial philosophical topics relatively often!

I intend to keep this passion project smaller. There need not be a feed to satisfy, algorithm to appease, nor a promise of a posting schedule. The genuine amibition is to leave some thoughts in a form that might assist my working memory and provide time for reflection. So, if anyone stumbles upon this site, I am most humbled that you decide to spend time reading about my thoughts!
