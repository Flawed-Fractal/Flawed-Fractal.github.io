Life and math

Novemeber 22, 2026 1-2 min read

Life has been quite messy! A lot of misgivings have taken me away from studies and distracted me a great deal. So, I need to consult with advisors and professors, though this post is more aptly motivated by the General Riemann Hypothesis having a claimed solution, as per the Levent Alpöge's directing Anthropic LLMs.

To briefly provide context, I am nearly 20, hence I feel as though it is extra unfortunate that I have been in free-fall! Doing subpar in classes, and having a lot of tragedy occur around me, particularly with bereavement and the health of immediate family, namely my younger brother suffering from a very storied situation that arose from a congenital condition. Mixed with identity and family, alongside sickness and my own health, and I believe I concluded earlier that I have been a bit unwell: not a satisfactory conclusion by any means. In that, I will likely see options currently and talk with some people around university tomorrow, Monday.

The state of mathematics seems to reflect the mess that I am. Not only my own studies, or lack thereof, but academia currently is in free fall: the greatest results have been proved by Machines. The proverbial "Move 37," I recall hearing some mathematician exclaim has occurred: I do not think anyone can argue. Thus, the widespread implications are quite tricky: what does this mean for the future of academics and studies?

I find myself asking, "What do I want?" I am unsure, in earnst, though I know that I am occasionally curious. I want to understand the lovely things that exist within the realm of math! So, I will try more-often to update here if I have free time and digest some of the hectic results that have recently been published in the world of math. We shall see!
