An empty notebook, deliberately

August 13, 2026 2 min read

A new website creates a dangerous temptation: to spend weeks perfecting its machinery before writing a sentence worth placing inside it. This one begins with the opposite constraint. It is nearly all text, and it already works.

I wanted somewhere quieter than a social feed and less ephemeral than a private note. The subjects will probably resist a coherent taxonomy—mathematics beside embedded systems, medicine beside philosophy, practical construction beside speculative thought. That disorder is part of the point.

The site may acquire machinery later. It must first acquire writing.

For now, the rule is simple: publish when turning an idea into an explanation teaches me something, or when the result may be useful to somebody else. Everything beyond that is decoration.
