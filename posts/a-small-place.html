An empty notebook

August 13, 2026 2 min read

In truth, this site exists as a testimony to procrastination. In staving off a pile of work to complete, I find the idea of creating a "blog" as a creative outlet, hence the apparent existence of this text. However, the interest has perambulated about my mind for quite some time.

My younger self prolifically wrote poems: hundreds of my poems populate some Google Docs. Throughout high school, life's misgivings subjected me to my fair share of adversity, and I found poetry as a fulfilling respite. As life progressed, the notion that my heavyhearted poems of hardship might offer empathy and companionship would periodically bounce about. Despite that, I never posted these poems, partially as I have drifted away from writing poems over the past two years: ironically, these years have also featured my most poignant tribulations thus far.

Though adolescent me sowed the seeds, two quasi-professional blogs, of which I frequently engage, prompted this site's creation: Terry Tao's WordPress and Bentham Bulldog's SubStack. For the unfamiliar, the first of these authors is one of the world's most accomplished mathematicians; the ladder is a philosophy graduate of the university I attend. I especially enjoy reading Bentham Bulldog's writings, as — though I oftentimes find qualms with his conclusions — he writes very well-articulated, thoughtful articles that I greatly enjoy. I follow Terence Tao's posts as an aspiring mathematician: apt analogy could depict a college basketball player listening to Michael Jordan's conversations about basketball.

The minute you get away from fundamentals—whether it’s proper technique, work ethic or mental preparation—the bottom can fall out of your game, your schoolwork, your job, whatever you’re doing. - Michael Jordan

For now, the rule is simple: publish when turning an idea into an explanation teaches me something, or when the result may be useful to somebody else. Everything beyond that is decoration.
