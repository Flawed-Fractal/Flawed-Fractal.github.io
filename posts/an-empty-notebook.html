An empty notebook

August 13, 2026 3-4 min read

In truth, this site exists as a testimony to procrastination. In staving off a pile of work to complete, I find the idea of creating a "blog" as a creative outlet, hence the apparent existence of this text. However, the interest has perambulated about my mind for quite some time.

My younger self prolifically wrote poems: hundreds of my poems populate some Google Docs. Throughout high school, life's misgivings subjected me to my fair share of adversity, and I found poetry as a fulfilling respite. As life progressed, the notion that my heavyhearted poems of hardship might offer empathy and companionship would periodically bounce about. Despite that, I never posted these poems, partially as I have drifted away from writing poems over the past two years: ironically, these years have also featured my most poignant tribulations thus far.

Though adolescent me sowed the seeds, two quasi-professional blogs, of which I frequently engage, prompted this site's creation: Terry Tao's WordPress and Bentham Bulldog's SubStack. For the unfamiliar, the first of these authors is one of the world's most accomplished mathematicians; the ladder is a philosophy graduate of the university I attend. I especially enjoy reading Bentham Bulldog's writings, as — though I oftentimes find qualms with his conclusions — he writes very well-articulated, thoughtful articles that I greatly enjoy. I follow Terence Tao's posts as an aspiring mathematician: apt analogy could depict a college basketball player listening to Michael Jordan's conversations about basketball, listening to each word as gospel:

I can accept failure. Everyone fails at something. But I can’t accept not trying.

Now, I know very little about sports, though Michael Jordan's aphorism offers both a transition into future posts and a final insight into this site. Aforementioned, life has been rough! Never do I wish for any blog to present as a diatribe which condemns life itself, though I find myself getting out of protracted stupor of sorts, for which I wish to cease the day. Moreover, for any failings and times lacking productivity, I am — as are you — human. Carpe Diem is to present me, as errare humanum est is to past me.

So, in lieu of more eulogizing of my intentions with this site, all subsequent posts will be suited towards specific topic matters. Hopefully this first post satiates any curiosity — both of myself and of others — as to the origins of this blog. Provided all of this context, I request all those reading, including myself, go about making today a fulfilling day! Whether that be reading some pages of a book, spending time with loved ones, or aiming to be effective in one's altruism, I entreat you to do all in your power to make today a good day!
